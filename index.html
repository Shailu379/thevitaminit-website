// About

Built on hands-on IT work, not a sales pitch.

The Vitamin IT is a Pune-based IT services practice built around one idea: small and mid-sized businesses deserve the same level of care as large companies, without the overhead of a big vendor.

It started with hands-on troubleshooting and freelance technical support, and has grown into a full practice covering IT support, networking, backups, security and managed AMC contracts — plus VitaVault, our own managed cloud platform for clients who want their data under one roof.

Every engagement is treated as a long-term relationship, not a ticket to close.
